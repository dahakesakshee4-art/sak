    Hook formulas

    The first line decides whether anyone reads the rest.

    • Mood lineSet the scene in one sensory sentence.Rain on the glass, espresso in hand.
    • Time stampAnchor the post to a specific moment.7pm to 1am, in five frames.
    • Direct addressSpeak to one specific person.First time at NOIR? Here's how to do it right.
    • POVPut the viewer inside a relatable moment.POV: you said 'one coffee' three hours ago.
    • Ranking / listPromise a quick, scannable payoff.Every NOIR regular at 11pm, ranked.
    • Before vs afterShow progress or contrast.Attempt 1 vs attempt 14.
    • Hot takeA mild opinion people want to react to.Coffee after 9pm is a personality.
    • Curiosity gapRaise a question the caption answers.Four minutes for one cup. Here's why.
    • Myth busterCorrect something people get wrong.Steamed, not scalded. There's a difference.

    Voice guide

    NOIR sounds unhurried, moody, precise and warm.

    • UnhurriedStay as long as you like.Grab your coffee and go!
    • MoodySoft lights, slow pours.The BEST vibes in town!!!
    • PreciseSilky milk sits around 60°C.Our coffee is simply amazing.
    • WarmThey know your order before you sit down.Valued customers, visit us today.

    Writing rules

    • Hook on the first line, under 10 words where possible.
    • Sentence case. No all-caps shouting, no strings of exclamation marks.
    • Short lines with a blank line between hook, body and CTA.
    • One CTA per caption, always last.
    • Emojis: none or one. The visuals carry the mood.
    • 3 to 5 hashtags: one branded (#NOIRafterdark) plus topic tags.
    • Never invent offers, prices or events that aren't confirmed.
