Gaps and NOIR's move

Each gap becomes a decision in the strategy.

1

Nobody owns the quiet evening

The gap. Most cafés post for the morning rush and bars post for loud nights.

NOIR's move. NOIR posts the hours between: slow pours, soft lights, open till 1am.

2

Craft is told only to experts

The gap. Specialty accounts explain coffee in jargon.

NOIR's move. One precise, plain fact per post, such as the temperature of the milk or why the bloom matters.

3

People appear at random

The gap. Staff and regulars show up when someone has a spare phone.

NOIR's move. A fixed People pillar with a weekly slot, so the faces become familiar.

4

Events have no repeatable format

The gap. Nights are announced once, then forgotten.

NOIR's move. The Friday listening session becomes a recurring series with the same look every week.

What I'd log before launch

The archetypes above are the working hypothesis. Before anything is published I'd audit five to eight real local accounts and record the same eight things for each.

  • Posts per week
  • Format mix (Reel, carousel, static, Story)
  • Share of posts with people in them
  • Caption length and tone
  • Call to action used
  • Comments per post, and who replies
  • Hashtag habits
  • Posting times against opening hours
