    How I built it

    1. Four pillars, no more than two days in a row from the same one, so the feed never feels like one note.
    2. Reels carry reach, carousels carry saves, Stories carry conversation, static posts carry mood.
    3. Every Friday is anchored by the listening session, so there is always a reason to come back that week.
    4. Every post has one job and one call to action. The wording sits in the caption bank.
    5. Nothing here invents offers, prices or events that aren't confirmed.
