How I work

Every project here follows the same path, so you can see the reasoning behind each piece of content.

  1. Audit

    Review the current feed, competitors and audience conversations.

  2. Insight

    Find what the audience is really buying into.

  3. Strategy

    Set positioning and the content pillars that carry it.

  4. Execution

    Reels, carousels, captions, stories and a calendar.

  5. Measurement

    Decide what success looks like before posting.

Brand positioning

Concept project

Case study 02

Social media strategy

Speculative, not commissioned by the brand

Elephant & Co., an independent social strategy concept. I chose this brand because its feed shows a common problem: great product, no reason to interact.

1

Before: the audit

Problem
Content lacks a clear recurring structure.
Observation
Most posts communicate the product but don't give the audience a reason to interact.
2

Insight

The audience isn't simply buying the product. They're buying the experience around it.

3

Strategy: four content pillars

CultureThe music, the nights, the moments the place is known for.
PeopleStaff, regulars and the faces that make it feel familiar.
ProductFood and drinks, shown in the context they're enjoyed.
ExperienceWhat an evening there actually feels like, start to finish.

Questions I get asked

What kind of roles are you looking for?

Social media, content and digital marketing roles, full-time or internship, plus freelance projects for brands that need a clear social strategy and content to go with it.

Have you worked with these brands?

Some projects here are independent strategic projects rather than commissioned client work. I created them to show my approach to brand positioning, social strategy and content.

Why no growth numbers?

I only share results I can back with analytics. For concept and speculative projects I show the metrics I would track instead.

Do you plan strategy or create the content too?

Both. I build the strategy and content pillars, then create the work: Reel concepts and storyboards, captions, Canva designs and short-form edits in CapCut.

How do you know if content is working?

I set KPIs before anything goes live. Reach and watch time show attention, saves and shares show value, and profile visits, enquiries and conversions show business impact. I review them regularly and adjust the calendar.

Can you handle paid social?

Yes. I plan paid campaigns alongside organic content in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads, and use CTR, CPC, CPM, ROAS and conversion rate to decide what to scale, fix or stop.

How do you keep up with trends?

I track Google Trends and AnswerThePublic, study what creators in the niche are posting, and only use a trend when it fits the brand's voice and audience.

What does a freelance project with you look like?

A short call about your goals and audience, then a social media audit. I share a strategy with content pillars and a calendar for your approval, create the content, and send a report with what's working and what to change.

Where are you based?

I'm based in Pune and open to on-site, hybrid and remote work.

Why isn't your client work shown here?

The work I've done for clients is covered by NDAs, so I can't share it publicly. Everything in this portfolio is independent work I created to show how I think and work. I'm happy to talk through my process and the kind of projects I've handled, without naming clients.
