NOIR, a concept café brand
Campaign
After dark
Duration
30 days: before opening to the first month
Prepared by
Sakshee Dahake

Objective

Make NOIR the first place people think of for a quiet evening out, and get them through the door in the first month.

Problem

A new café in a city full of good coffee needs a reason to be chosen, not just found.

Audience

22 to 30. Remote workers and postgraduate students who want a third place after 7pm that isn't a bar.

What they believe

Good coffee is easy to find. A calm place that stays open is not.

The idea

Every post answers the same question: what does it feel like to be here after dark?

After dark.

Single-minded propositionNOIR is the late-evening coffee bar that feels like a quiet night out, without the noise.

Plan

Three phases across four channels.

  1. Before openingTeaser posts from inside the fit-out and a waitlist for the first evening.
  2. Launch weekOpening night Reels, local creator visits and a first-week offer shown only on Instagram.
  3. First monthWeekly listening sessions become recurring content and a reason to return.

How success is measured

  • Reach and watch timeIs the idea getting attention?
  • Saves and sharesIs it worth keeping and passing on?
  • Profile visitsIs attention turning into curiosity?
  • Offer redemptions and map directionsIs curiosity turning into visits?
  • Repeat visitsIs NOIR becoming a habit?

Targets are set after a baseline is taken. This brief lists what would be tracked, not results.
