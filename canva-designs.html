Layout rules

  1. One idea per slide, twelve words at most.
  2. Wordmark top left on every slide, in the same place.
  3. Slide 1 asks the question. The last slide gives the call to action.
  4. Stories: keep the top and bottom 14% free of text so nothing sits under the interface.
  5. Light text on dark colours, dark text on light colours. No mixing.
  6. Swap colours every slide, but only within the palette.
